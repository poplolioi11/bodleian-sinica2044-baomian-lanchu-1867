# Methods

AI-assisted source-image initial transcription with fixed image hashes and physical-face IDs. Body text, small notes, raised words, running margins, source annotations and visible marks are distinguished where observed. Stable gaiji markers preserve unresolved glyphs and partial regions. Context was reused and includes bibliographic/previous-draft exposure; no independent blind collation has been applied. Full human double collation, expert review, peer review and accuracy measurement have not been performed. The actual execution-model identifier, reasoning setting and token counters are unknown, not zero. Format/schema/hash checks assess engineering consistency and do not prove reading accuracy. Source images, crops, dedicated programs, prompts and full private working records are not distributed; no complete image-to-text reproduction method is provided. Historical-document research only, with no modern clinical validation or biological research claim.
