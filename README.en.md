# 保免攔除（1867；Bodleian Sinica 2044） / Bao mian lan chu [Preventing and averting illness]: Transcription dataset

Bao mian lan chu [Preventing and averting illness] is an 1867 Chinese medical and everyday-health booklet by Frederick Porter Smith. Bodleian Libraries, Sinica 2044, is catalogued as published in Hankow (漢皋); its title page names Baoshan jushe (寶善居舍) as block holder. It discusses housing, diet, mental and physical activity, cleanliness, internal and external medicine, eye conditions, women’s health and infant care, linking health discourse with Christian vocabulary. It supports historical work on medical translation, missionary publishing and everyday life in late-Qing China. Based on 21 digital captures, TXT, JSON and TEI XML provide the transcription of 36 designated physical faces, uncertainties and source-image mappings. Following the AI initial transcription, this version applies a separate AI source-only reading of all 36 faces, full comparison and nonblind source-image adjudication. Full human double collation and expert review have not been performed; unresolved readings and clipped marginal material remain marked.

Prepared by Chu, Chen-Yu ([ORCID](https://orcid.org/0009-0003-9738-6914)); account poplolioi11. Version 0.2.0.
