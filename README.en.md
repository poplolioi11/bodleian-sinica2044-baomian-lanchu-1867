# 保免攔除（1867；Bodleian Sinica 2044） / Bao mian lan chu [Preventing and averting illness]: Transcription dataset

Bao mian lan chu [Preventing and averting illness] is an 1867 Chinese medical and everyday-health booklet by Frederick Porter Smith. Bodleian Libraries, Sinica 2044, is catalogued as published in Hankow (漢皋); its title page names Baoshan jushe (寶善居舍) as the block holder. It discusses housing, diet, mental and physical activity, cleanliness, internal and external medicine, eye conditions, women’s health and infant care, linking health discourse with Christian vocabulary. It supports historical work on medical translation, missionary publishing and everyday life in late-Qing China. Based on 21 Bodleian digital captures, this dataset provides an AI initial transcription of 36 designated physical faces, including titles, prefaces, main text, appendices, running margins and one originally blank framed body. TXT, JSON and TEI XML retain readings, uncertainties and source-image mappings. Full human double collation and expert review have not been performed; uncertain readings and clipped marginal material remain marked.

Prepared by Chu, Chen-Yu ([ORCID](https://orcid.org/0009-0003-9738-6914)); account poplolioi11.
