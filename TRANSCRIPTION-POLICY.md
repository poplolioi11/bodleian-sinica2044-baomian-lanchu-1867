# Transcription policy

Designated Bodleian witness only. Source columns read vertically top-to-bottom, right-to-left, right face before left. Retain selected codepoint strings, variants/suspected errors; no silent normalization, modernization or restoration from a standard text. Raised words stay in source column; small notes/annotations separated where recorded. Gaiji references retain unresolved shapes, partial margins and overwritten glyph. Separate visible small 食 on f014-R not silently applied to body. f014-L list keeps columnwise gaps using approximate spaces, without inferred row alignment. Source point/slant/circle marks have provisional encodings/undetermined origin; no critically punctuated reading layer supplied. Unknown glyph count is not one character; pending processing distinct from unresolved readings.
